This is the first Boss, Demon Tablet. Before we start keep the following in mind:

  • Make sure to use the Knight buff and only the Knight buff before jumping up

  • The raid will be mostly split in 2 groups ABC and DEF

  • DEF will alway go through to the numbers side for relevant tower and add mechanics

  • Do not target the boss until explanations and ready checks are done, this is to prevent early pulls

  • The first damage will be a heavy raidwide, so mitigate shortly before or on pull to prevent immediate causalities

Ray of Expulsion Afar / Danger's Near (Near/Far)

  • Avoid the side that shows three red orbs, that side will encounter a heavy hitting AOE that usually kills

  • The first time you encounter this mechanic walk through the wall, then through again on the second cast

  • To be on the safe side, always do the opposite of the cast name.

Danger's Near means you have to stand far. Expulsion Afar means you have to be close.

  • The phSamurai's ability "Shirahadori" can negate the damage and ruin stack from this mechanic, use this to gain uptime on any "Danger's Near" cast to gain uptime.
